The Facade Design Pattern is a structural design pattern that provides a simple interface to a complex system.

In simple words: Facade hides the complexity and gives the client an easy way to use a system.

Easy interview definition

Facade Pattern provides a simple interface to a complex subsystem by hiding the complexity of multiple classes.

Remember:
Facade = Simple front door to a complex system.

Simple Real-Life Example

Example: Online Shopping

Suppose buying a product requires three steps:

Check product
Make payment
Ship product

Without Facade, the client has to call all three classes.
